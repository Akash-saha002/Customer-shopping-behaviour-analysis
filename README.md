# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **customer shopping behaviour** using **SQL, Python, and Power BI** to extract meaningful insights from customer purchase data.

The analysis focuses on customer spending, product performance, discounts, shipping preferences, subscription status, reviews, and previous purchase behaviour.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze total revenue based on gender
* Identify customers who made purchases above the average purchase amount while using discounts
* Find products with the highest average review ratings
* Compare average spending between Express and Standard shipping
* Compare spending and revenue between subscribers and non-subscribers
* Identify products with the highest purchase rate among discounted purchases
* Analyze repeat customer behaviour and subscription status

---

## 🔍 SQL Analysis

The SQL analysis includes:

### 1. Revenue by Gender

Analyzed total revenue generated by different genders.

### 2. Discount & Above-Average Purchases

Identified customers who received a discount and whose purchase amount was higher than the overall average purchase amount.

### 3. Top Products by Review Rating

Found the top 5 products based on average review rating.

### 4. Shipping Type Analysis

Compared the average purchase amount between **Express** and **Standard** shipping.

### 5. Subscription Analysis

Compared subscribers and non-subscribers based on:

* Total customers
* Total revenue
* Average spending

### 6. Discounted Product Purchase Rate

Identified the top 5 products based on their purchase rate among discounted purchases.

### 7. Repeat Customer Analysis

Analyzed customers with more than 5 previous purchases and examined their subscription status.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **Customer Behaviour Dashboard** was created in Power BI to visualize customer demographics, purchasing patterns, revenue performance, subscription behaviour, payment methods, and product category insights.

### 🔹 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes three main KPIs:

- **Total Customers:** 2.79K
- **Total Revenue:** $166.11K
- **Average Purchase Amount:** $59.54

### 🔹 Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive slicers to analyze customer behaviour based on:

- **Gender:** Female / Male
- **Age Group:** Adult / Middle-age / Senior
- **Season:** Fall / Spring / Summer / Winter

These filters allow users to dynamically explore the dashboard and analyze different customer segments.

### 🔹 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

#### 1. Customer Subscription Status
A donut chart showing the percentage of customers who are subscribed and non-subscribed.

- **Subscribed:** 27.71%
- **Non-subscribed:** 72.29%

#### 2. Total Customers by Category
A column chart showing the number of customers across different product categories:

- Clothing
- Accessories
- Footwear
- Outerwear

#### 3. Revenue by Category
A bar chart comparing total purchase revenue generated by each product category.

#### 4. Most Used Payment Method
A horizontal bar chart showing customer usage across different payment methods, including:

- Credit Card
- PayPal
- Cash
- Venmo

#### 5. Average Review Rating by Category
A donut chart comparing the average review rating across product categories, including:

- Footwear
- Accessories

- Outerwear


The combination of **KPIs, slicers, charts, and category-level analysis** makes the dashboard useful for exploring customer behaviour and identifying business-relevant patterns.
